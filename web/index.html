Experimental

Only what they see or hear: bots spot you only on their screen, in front of them and in a clear line; behind them, they only hear you (shots, explosions, jetpacks, landings, running close by; creeping along crouched is silent). Fog of war: you only see what your soldier can see; the rest is greyed out, anyone there is hidden (teammates too, though big maps' minimap still shows them), and sounds from there show as orange ripples (or arrows at the screen's edge). Online, the host's choice counts. Also in Settings.

This game needs a keyboard and mouse.

Keys

Click a key to change it, then press the new key or mouse button (Esc to keep it as it was, Backspace to clear it). A key taken from another action leaves that one.

Mouse

Mouse pointerYour system's pointer, drawn as the crosshair, moves exactly as your mouse does everywhere else, with no delay (the system moves it, not the game). In a window it can leave the game: full screen keeps it on the screen. Locked to the game, the pointer can't leave, but the browser hides it, so the game draws the crosshair, a frame or so behind. Either way, while you play the browser keeps out of it: no menus, zooming or text selection, whatever keys you hold. Esc opens the menu (and frees a locked mouse).
