Experimental

Only what they see or hear: bots spot you only on their screen, in front of them and in a clear line; behind them, they only hear you (shots, explosions, jetpacks, landings, running close by; creeping along crouched is silent). Fog of war: you only see what your soldier can see; the rest is greyed out, enemies there are hidden, and sounds from there show as orange ripples (or arrows at the screen's edge). Also in Settings.

This game needs a keyboard and mouse.
